## Dock display

Q66 reports intermittent multiple non-contact bubbles. Fixed-f5 actual InputSystem replay separates two paths: spring tails overlap while the current target is one; a PressModel historical contact latch allows a separated tip to bypass the group slot. Neither synthetic case proves every device occurrence.

Keep the action/contact history, release/cancel/loss and independent selections unchanged. A separated tip, including a still-acquired press, competes for the single nearest/stable proximity slot. Only the current slot, current physical contacts and captured ray presses show bubble/name/associated glyph feedback. Losing feedback is masked rather than painted as another hover during its internal recovery. Spatial neighbour magnification is unchanged. Actual multiple contacts remain independent.

## Lower sphere mark removal

A bounded normal-distance fixed-f5 comparison separates explicit lip/glint/shadow/core/ring from the local well's geometry/normal lighting. Turning off only explicit light leaves a bright circular mark; omitting the local well as well removes that mark. Lower sphere opts into deformation-only rendering: retain its accepted affine compression, axis, pressure, recovery, body opacity, rest size/position and ordinary reflected/key/rim lighting, without the stamped local hollow or its additional lighting. No Current/plate/icon/global material change. Surviving secondary physical pressure contributes to whole-body deformation after primary loss without acquiring actions or manipulation; a held grab retains its own shape. Contact state and capture remain intact; contact-well uniforms are retained for diagnostics but no longer draw point wells on these opted-in controls.

## Existing window controls

Actual source mapping supersedes the initial shorthand: side move is a rounded rectangular slab; corner resize is a sphere with the existing mirrored arrow. Keep both original rest meshes, size, positions, arrow, pinch manipulation, one gesture owner, hover OR held reveal, release-hover and held window opacity.

Side body compresses along the front normal against its fixed back plane and spreads in X/Y. Corner body flattens along the actual press/optional pinch axis about its own centre, without the hanging sphere's stem offset. Positive affine transforms have determinant one. Compression is a trial .95 times physical depth divided by actual local thickness/radius, bounded at .25. Existing world 2 mm press / 1.5 mm held feedback floors remain; input scale is applied once. Existing optional-axis/loss/recovery policy is reused. Pressure acquisition retains its rest reference so body growth cannot create a new press; secondary proximity follows the deformed front/ellipsoid and rays use the actual transformed mesh. The rectangular proximity domain remains the pre-existing front-face rectangle, not a claim of exact bevel collision.

The same transform is applied to shader vertices/normals, ray hit geometry and arrow (including its existing left/right mirror). Controls use whole-body feedback without extra local stamped wells or point lighting. Normal/reduced restoration, scale endpoints, actual InputSystem ownership and surface alignment are required. A legacy ownership fixture that recomputed the fingertip from a deforming mesh now holds a fixed world point; otherwise the fixture itself moves the captured hand.
